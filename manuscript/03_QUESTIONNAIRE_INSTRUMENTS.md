## SECTION C: HOUSEHOLD FOOD INSECURITY ACCESS SCALE (HFIAS)

**Introductory statement (read verbatim):**

> Now I am going to ask you some questions about your household's access to food. These questions relate to the past four weeks (30 days).

| Code | Question | Occurrence (0–3) | Coping (0–3) |
|------|----------|------------------|--------------|
| | **Occurrence:** 0=Never, 1=Rarely, 2=Sometimes, 3=Often | | |
| | **Coping:** 0=No, 1=Rarely, 2=Sometimes, 3=Often (only if occurrence >0 for Q2–9) | | |
| H1 | Did you worry that your household would not have enough food? | ___ | N/A |
| H2 | Were you or any household member unable to eat preferred foods because of lack of resources? | ___ | ___ |
| H3 | Did you or any household member have to eat a limited variety of foods due to lack of resources? | ___ | ___ |
| H4 | Did you or any household member have to eat some foods that you really did not want to eat because of lack of resources? | ___ | ___ |
| H5 | Did you or any household member have to eat a smaller meal than you felt you needed because of lack of food? | ___ | ___ |
| H6 | Did you or any household member have to eat fewer meals in a day because there was not enough food? | ___ | ___ |
| H7 | Was there ever no food to eat in your household because of lack of resources? | ___ | ___ |
| H8 | Did you or any household member go to sleep at night hungry because there was not enough food? | ___ | ___ |
| H9 | Did you or any household member go a whole day and night without eating anything because there was not enough food? | ___ | ___ |

**Scoring (for data entry):**
- HFIAS occurrence score = sum of occurrence codes (H1–H9): range 0–27
- HFIAS category (software algorithm per Coates et al., 2007):
  - 0: Food secure
  - 1–10: Mildly insecure
  - 11–20: Moderately insecure
  - 21–27: Severely insecure

## SECTION G: KEY INFORMANT INTERVIEW GUIDE (n = 15)

**Participants:** Cooperative leaders (5), extension agents (5), market women leaders (3), nutrition/public health officers (2)

**Core questions:**
1. How do smallholder farmers in Ilorin currently access markets for fresh produce?
2. What are the main barriers to household food security in this metropolis?
3. How might digital traceability (QR codes) influence consumer trust and food purchasing?
4. What is your experience with GroChain or similar platforms?
5. How do financial inclusion tools (mobile payments, BVN) affect farmer participation?
6. What role can Home Economics extension play in linking digital tools to nutrition education?
7. What policy changes would maximise nutrition-sensitive outcomes from digital agriculture?

**Probes:** USSD access, gender equity, post-harvest loss, lean season vulnerability, youth engagement.
