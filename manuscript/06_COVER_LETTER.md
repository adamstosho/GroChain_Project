**Re: Submission of original research manuscript — "A nutrition-sensitive digital food trust model and household food security in Ilorin Metropolis, Kwara State, Nigeria: A mixed-methods evaluation of the GroChain integrative platform"**

We are pleased to submit our original research article for consideration in *World Nutrition*. This manuscript presents a mixed-methods evaluation examining associations between participation in the GroChain nutrition-sensitive digital food trust platform and household food insecurity (HFIAS) and minimum dietary diversity (MDD-W) among smallholder farmers and urban food buyers in Ilorin Metropolis, Kwara State, Nigeria.

**Why World Nutrition is the appropriate venue**

This study sits at the intersection of public health nutrition, food systems, and equity—core domains of your journal's scope. Unlike conventional agtech evaluations that report yield or adoption alone, we measure **household food security and dietary diversity** as primary outcomes, aligning with the World Public Health Nutrition Association's mission to address biological, social, economic, and environmental determinants of nutrition across populations. The study is conducted in a peri-urban Nigerian setting where 41% of children are stunted (2024 NDHS) and digital agriculture policy is rapidly expanding without nutrition-sensitive evaluation frameworks.

**Novelty and significance**

To our knowledge, this is among the first empirical evaluations in West Africa to test an **integrated** digital platform combining QR traceability, marketplace access, BVN-linked financial inclusion, and USSD offline channels against validated food security and dietary diversity metrics. The work is led by Home Economics faculty and contributes actionable evidence for extension practice, Kwara State policy, and Nigeria's National Digital Agriculture Strategy.
